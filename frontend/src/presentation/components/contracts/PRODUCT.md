# Fleetum — Emissione manuale e firma

Extension of the approved operational Fleetum UI. Keep its soft surfaces, restrained blue accent, typography, compact finite notifications, responsive composition and light/dark themes. Login and public website are outside scope. No new visual direction.

An operator reviews party/vehicle, expressly agreed gross EUR price, optional discount provenance, confirmed receipts and outstanding balance, then manually issues the contract. Zero, partial receipts and unpaid balances are valid. Deposits and cards are separate. A draft is technical preparation, never an issued or signed agreement. Price overrides require existing finance-charge permission; ordinary price-list quotation retains its existing permission. Historical missing provenance is never invented.

The manual issuance is atomic per booking, validates a fresh review fingerprint and does not duplicate booking, rental or receipts. The CARGOS prompt opens after manual issuance, with an explicit user action and honest unavailable/provider status. No automatic transmission.

Signing offers email OTP or in-person graphical capture after the exact version is opened. A scoped HttpOnly browser proof, explicit consent, signer identity, immutable original hash, signed copy hash, version and timestamps bind the result. The canvas supports pointer/touch; email is the non-drawing alternative. Loading, missing configuration, expiry, revocation, failure and signed status remain distinct. Originals, legacy signed copies and terminal evidence are preserved; later receipts cannot rewrite them. Substantive changes while signing require revocation/new preparation; signed content cannot be overwritten. Before handover, an explicit new revision requires both original and signed stored bytes to pass their integrity checks, preserves every signed version and requires fresh issuance/signing. After handover, a separate act is required; historical signatures without an exact preserved original are not silently reconstructed. Price changes after an express agreement go through its audited editor. Later money movements never rewrite the signed PDF.

This block implements the workflow and evidence pages; detailed professional contract layout/pagination is block 5. Temporary PostgreSQL and synthetic data only. No real email, payments, CARGOS sends, provider configuration, push, merge, staging or production deployment.
