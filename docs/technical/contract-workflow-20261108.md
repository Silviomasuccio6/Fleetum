## Behavior and authority

A generated template is a technical draft, labelled BOZZA in its PDF. It is not
commercial issuance or signature. Existing historical records are not backfilled,
reclassified or regenerated by the migration.

`GET /rental-bookings/:id/contract/conversion` returns a tenant/site-scoped review:
party, vehicle, dates/locations, readiness issues, agreed price, receipts, due and
credit. The financial source is the existing collection ledger. A fresh review hash
must be confirmed at `POST .../contract/convert`; the booking lock makes concurrent
requests produce one issuance event and one existing contract, without duplicating
booking, rental or receipts. Zero, discounts, partial receipts and open balances are
allowed. Unknown totals remain unknown. No payment/card/deposit triggers conversion.

`PUT .../contract/agreed-price` requires vehicles:write and the existing
rental-payments:charge permission (ADMIN behavior unchanged). Integer gross EUR
list/discount amounts, a reason, actor and timestamp are recorded. The agreed
booking total remains authoritative; old snapshots never invent discount provenance.
Existing price setters cannot overwrite an express agreement or issued contract.

The UI preserves unsaved rental terms and document text when saving price. It
blocks issuance/signing until those local drafts are saved. Terms reload only for
an actual document revision, not for ordinary price refreshes. Financial/history
values are protected from DOM translation; IT/EN values are checked in the browser.

## Signature and delivery

Both methods preserve the exact original PDF, its SHA-256, source fingerprint,
version, actor/site authority and expiration. The customer opens that version;
a scoped HttpOnly, strict same-site proof binds its hash and browser to consent.

Email OTP retains expiration, cooldown, send/attempt limits, provider acknowledgment,
one-use consumption, revocation and exact-retry behavior. Wrong-code attempts are
committed. A separate signed copy appends evidence to the preserved pages; original
and final checksums are stored separately. Email delivery states remain distinct
from signing and from confirmed receipts. A new signing key alone is not reported
as a configured email transport.

In-person signing uses private authenticated/CSRF-protected prepare/document/confirm
routes, the preparing operator's current authority, explicit consent and a bounded,
nonblank PNG. The signed PDF contains the captured image and evidence. No email is
invented. Concurrent confirms record one signature/copy. Signature/status shortcuts
(mark-signed, booking transition/status and contract patch) cannot bypass this flow.
Booking, contract monitoring and customer history all lead to the same workflow.
This is a record of the implemented process, not a claim of qualified signature.

Pre-upgrade email invitations may use their original fingerprint only when the
old-format request hash matches, revision is zero, no new price agreement exists,
and the agreed amount has not changed. Their original bytes are never rewritten;
a changed total is still rejected. Their completion does not fabricate a historical
manual-issuance event.

## Revisions, history and CARGOS

Before handover, `POST .../contract/revision` starts an explicit new revision only
after both prior artifacts pass ownership, metadata and byte-integrity checks.
Historical signed versions remain immutable and available from scoped version-PDF
routes. New terms require new issuance and a new signature. After handover a separate
act is required; legacy signatures lacking an exact preserved copy are not rebuilt.
Later receipts/refunds cannot overwrite signed artifacts. Old ordinary email jobs
are blocked when their contract revision is superseded; signed-copy delivery remains
bound to the original signed request. Privacy export includes price provenance and
non-secret signing metadata, excluding tokens, OTP/browser hashes and encrypted mail.

The CARGOS dialog opens after explicit manual issuance; sending remains a separate
operator action and missing configuration is honest. Its contract date uses issuedAt,
with signedAt as the historical fallback. A later OTP/graphical signature alone must
not invalidate accepted transmission of unchanged data. No payment/signing prerequisite
is invented for the new issuance event. The official manual describes communication
at stipulation sufficiently before handover; this implementation is not external
legal/provider certification: [official manual](https://cargos.poliziadistato.it/Cargos_Portale/Download/Manuali/p2.pdf).
Live CARGOS remains closed without the rental business's authorized API account.

## Migration, impact and rollback

New migration `20261107190000_manual_contract_issuance` adds booking price provenance,
contract issuance/revision metadata, signing method/revision and signed-file references.
The final-file FK includes tenantId. Original immutability and terminal-state triggers
remain; method/revision are immutable. Email OTP retains its consumed-code constraint;
in-person signatures instead require document-view time, consent, image hash and a
preserved final artifact. Existing email rows use revision zero / EMAIL_OTP; no history
or PDFs are converted. New objects consume the existing tenant storage quota.

Deployment must pair the backend and frontend because old signature shortcuts now
return an explicit 409. No downgrade SQL drops evidence. Before any new signatures,
a code rollback can retain these additive columns. After new signatures/revisions,
retain schema and objects and use a compatible reader/forward fix; reverting blindly
to the baseline would reopen insecure shortcuts and lose correct display of new
proof. Any exceptional rollback must first freeze affected writes and preserve both
artifacts, hashes, events and the ledger. No restore or rollback was executed here.
